# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

This policy describes how the iOS app "Zunzun" (the "App") handles your information.

## 1. Our Approach

**The App does not send your information to the developer or to any third-party server.** The App has no server and no accounts. Your walks, photos, and settings are stored only on your device.

## 2. Information Handled on Your Device

| Information | Purpose | Where it's stored |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Recording your route, distance, and time; detecting checkpoints | On your device (App data) |
| Photos taken with the camera or chosen from your library | Creating Cards | On your device (App data) |
| Cards you create | Saved to Photos when you tap Save to Photos | Photos app |
| Settings (checkpoint interval, distance unit, etc.) | Running the App | On your device |

- Location is used only between tapping Walk and tapping Finish (recording continues with the screen off).
- To show place names, the App may send a location (latitude and longitude) to Apple's map service (MapKit). This is subject to Apple's privacy policy. Maps are also displayed using Apple's map service.
- Deleting a walk removes its route and the photos stored in the App from your device. Cards saved to Photos are not deleted.
- Deleting the App removes all walks and photos stored in it.

## 3. Information Collected by the Developer

The developer does not collect any of your information. The App contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking tools.

## 4. Purchases

In-app purchases and payments are handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your name or payment information. Purchase status is checked on your device through Apple's StoreKit.

## 5. Notifications

Checkpoint notifications are local notifications created on your device. They are not sent from any external server.

## 6. Support Inquiries

If you contact us by email, your email address and message are used only to respond to your inquiry.

## 7. Changes

Any changes to this policy will be posted on this page.
