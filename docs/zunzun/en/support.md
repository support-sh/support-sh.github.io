# Zunzun Support

Turn your walk into a card. This is the support page for Zunzun.

## Walking Safely (Please Read)

1. **Don't look at your screen while walking** — When a notification arrives, stop somewhere safe before taking a photo
2. **Watch out for heat, cold, and hydration** — On long walks, rest often and drink plenty of water
3. **Stay out of restricted areas** — Don't enter private property, restricted areas, or dangerous places
4. **Mind your battery** — Recording uses location continuously, which uses more battery. A backup battery is a good idea

## FAQ

**Q: Does it record with the screen off?**
A: Yes. From the moment you tap Walk until you tap Finish, Zunzun keeps recording even with the screen off. A location indicator appears at the top of the screen.

**Q: Does train or car travel count?**
A: No. Stretches where you keep moving faster than 15 km/h are treated as vehicle travel. They aren't counted and are drawn as a dotted line.

**Q: I'm not getting notifications**
A: Allow notifications for Zunzun in the Settings app. While the app is open, you'll be alerted on screen instead.

**Q: Do I need to allow location "Always"?**
A: No. "While Using the App" is enough to keep recording with the screen off.

**Q: I'm worried about battery drain**
A: Recording uses location continuously, which uses more battery. On long walks, bring a backup battery. You can also pause while you rest.

**Q: Where are my walks and photos stored?**
A: Only on this iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere. Cards are saved to Photos only when you tap Save to Photos.

**Q: Can I move my walks to a new iPhone?**
A: No. Walks are stored only on the device. Save any cards you want to keep to Photos.

**Q: What's the difference between the Full Set and the Day Pass?**
A: Both unlock the Survey and Margin styles, finishing options, high-resolution export, and the all-time route map. The Full Set is a one-time purchase that lasts forever; the Day Pass lasts 24 hours from purchase. Neither ever renews or charges you again.

**Q: I bought it but can't use it**
A: Try Restore Purchases in Settings. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to purchase.
