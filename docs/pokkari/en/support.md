# Pokkari - Support

## About the App

Pokkari cleans up photos to free up space on your iPhone. It groups screenshots, bursts, large videos and more, and sorts them by how much space they free. Photos to clean up are already checked, so you just tap the ones you want to keep. All analysis happens on your iPhone, and your photos are never sent anywhere.

## Features

- **Biggest savings first** — screenshots, screen recordings, note photos, bursts, similar photos, and large videos
- **No picking one by one** — photos to clean up are pre-checked; for bursts and similar photos, the best shot is kept
- **Undo for 30 days** — cleaned-up photos stay in Recently Deleted in the Photos app for 30 days
- **Pokkari Pro (one-time purchase)** — unlimited cleanup, similar photos, and Weekend Tidy-Up reminders

## Requirements

- iPhone with iOS 27.0 or later

## About Pokkari Pro

Pokkari Pro is a **one-time purchase**, not a subscription, so you won't be charged again. On devices with the same Apple Account, use "Restore Purchases." Family Sharing is supported.

## FAQ

### Q: I cleaned up photos, but my free space didn't increase

A: Cleaned-up photos move to Recently Deleted in the Photos app and are removed after 30 days, which frees the space. To free it now, go to Albums → Recently Deleted in the Photos app and delete them.

### Q: I cleaned up a photo by mistake

A: You can recover it from Recently Deleted in the Photos app within 30 days.

### Q: My favorite photos don't appear as suggestions

A: Photos marked as Favorites are excluded from cleanup suggestions.

### Q: It says "Checking selected photos only"

A: Photo access is set to Limited. To check all photos, go to Settings → Apps → Pokkari → Photos and choose Full Access.

### Q: Why does it say "Up to about"?

A: Photos that aren't on your device (such as photos only in iCloud) have unknown sizes and aren't counted. The amount also changes if you change which photos to clean up.

### Q: What can I do with the free version?

A: You can find suggestions and see how much space you can free. You can clean up to 100 photos a day.

### Q: I'd like a refund

A: Refunds are subject to Apple's refund policy. Please request one from your App Store purchase history.

## Notes

- Suggestions are chosen automatically and may include photos you want to keep. Please review the list before cleaning up
- We recommend backing up important photos before cleaning up
