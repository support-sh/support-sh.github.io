# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the iOS app "Pokkari" (the "App") handles user information.

## 1. Our Approach

The App does not require an account. **Photos and videos in your photo library, and the results of analyzing them, are processed and stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer's servers or to third parties.** The developer cannot see your photos.

The App contains no tracking or analytics.

## 2. Information the App Handles

### 2.1 Photo Library

- To find cleanup suggestions, the App reads the photos and videos in your photo library and their information (date taken, type, whether they are screenshots or bursts, and file size)
- Photo content is used only for on-device image analysis (Apple's Vision) to judge image quality, whether a photo is a document or note, and whether photos are similar
- You can change or revoke photo access at any time in the Settings app

### 2.2 Information Stored on Your Device

- To avoid re-analyzing photos every time, the App stores photo identifiers and analysis results (numbers) on your device (SwiftData). It does not store copies of your photos
- Nothing is synced to iCloud or uploaded to external servers

### 2.3 Cleaning Up Photos

- The App deletes only the photos you choose and approve in the iOS confirmation. Deleted photos are moved to Recently Deleted in the Photos app

### 2.4 Notifications

- Only if you turn on Weekend Tidy-Up, a weekly notification is scheduled on your device. No information is sent anywhere for notifications

## 3. Network Communication

- **In-app purchase**: The Unlimited Pack is purchased through Apple's StoreKit, and payment is processed by Apple. The developer does not receive or store payment information
- **Displaying photos**: If you use iCloud Photos, iOS may load photos from iCloud when they are shown on screen. This is handled by iOS, and no information is sent to the developer
- Other than the above, the App does not communicate externally

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties

Because the App does not send your information to the developer, the developer does not share it with third parties, except where disclosure is required by law.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not send personal information anywhere.

## 6. Deleting Data

- Deleting the App from your device deletes all analysis results stored in the App. Photos in your photo library are not deleted

## 7. Changes to This Policy

The developer may change this Privacy Policy as needed. Changes take effect when posted on this page. If a change expands the information handled, we will announce it in the app's update notes or similar.
