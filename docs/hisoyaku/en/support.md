# HisoYaku – Support

## About the App

HisoYaku translates entirely on your iPhone. All translation happens on the device, and the App does not connect to the internet. Neither your original text nor the translation is ever sent anywhere, and no account is needed.

The translation engine is "PLaMo 3 Translate," a translation language model published by Preferred Networks, Inc. (Built with PLaMo). HisoYaku is made by an independent developer and is not provided or endorsed by Preferred Networks.

## Features

- **On-device translation** — Works without a connection, even in Airplane Mode
- **Glossary** — Register how company names, product names, and technical terms should be translated, and keep them consistent
- **Japanese style** — When translating into Japanese, choose Default, Polite (desu/masu), or Plain (da/de aru) style
- **Context** — Add a description of the situation or surrounding sentences to get translations that fit
- **Long text, paragraph by paragraph** — Each paragraph is translated using the previous paragraph's translation as a hint, so terms and tone stay more consistent
- **53 languages** — Including English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified and Traditional), and Korean
- **Text from photos** — Recognize text in a photo on your device and translate it
- **History** — Your translations are kept on your device
- **Shortcuts** — Translate with the "Translate with HisoYaku" action in the Shortcuts app

## Requirements

- iPhone with iOS 27.0 or later
- Because the translation model is included, the App is a large download and needs free storage on your device

## Price

Free. There are no in-app purchases.

## Getting Around

- The tabs at the bottom are Translate, History, Glossary, and Settings
- The first time you open the App, a three-page introduction appears. Tap Next to continue, then tap "Agree and Start" on the last page to begin

## FAQ

### Q: Does it really not use the internet?

A: Yes. The translation model is built into the App, and all translation happens on your iPhone. It works the same way in Airplane Mode.

### Q: Why is the App so large?

A: It includes the translation model (about 570 MB). In return, it's ready to use as soon as it's installed, with no model to download later.

### Q: How do I keep terms consistent?

A: In the Glossary tab, tap + (Add Term) at the top right, enter a term and its translation, and tap Save (for example, dashboard → 管理画面). When a registered term appears in your text, the App uses your translation. Use the switch next to each term to turn it on or off, and swipe left to delete it.

### Q: A word is translated in a way that doesn't fit the situation

A: In the Translate tab, open Context and describe the situation or add the surrounding sentences (for example, "A walk by the river"). The App uses it as a hint.

### Q: Long text takes a while to translate

A: Long text is translated one paragraph at a time. The translation appears gradually, so you can start reading before it finishes. While it's translating, the Translate button changes to Stop, so you can also stop it partway.

### Q: Text in my photo isn't recognized well

A: Tap "Read from Photo" below the input field and choose a photo; the recognized text is placed in the input field. Text recognition uses iOS's built-in text recognition, so it supports only the languages iOS supports. Small, tilted, or blurry text can be hard to read. You can correct the recognized text in the input field before translating.

### Q: Are my photos saved?

A: No. Photos are used only to recognize the text in them.

### Q: How do I delete my history?

A: In the History tab, swipe left to delete one translation, or tap Delete All at the top right. You can also use Delete All History in the Settings tab.

### Q: Can I use it with Shortcuts?

A: Yes. Add the "Translate with HisoYaku" action in the Shortcuts app. You can set the text and the language to translate into, and the action returns the translation. If you don't set the language, it translates into English when your iPhone's language is Japanese, and into Japanese otherwise. Enabled glossary terms are used, too. Open the App once first and go through the introduction to "Agree and Start."

### Q: My history and glossary are gone after changing phones

A: History and the glossary are stored only on the device, so they don't transfer when you change phones or delete the App.

### Q: Can I post translations on my blog or social media?

A: Yes. When you do, please note that the text was translated by a language model (PLaMo). This is a condition of the PLaMo Community License.

### Q: Where can I read the PLaMo Community License?

A: In the Settings tab, go to Licenses and tap PLaMo Community License to read the full text.

## Important Notes

- Translations are generated automatically by a language model and may contain errors. For legal, medical, financial, or other important decisions, don't rely on a translation alone; check the original text or consult a professional
- If you are under 18, please get permission from a parent or guardian before using the App
