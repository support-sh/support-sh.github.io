# Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "HisoYaku" (the "App") handles your information.

## 1. Our Approach

The App does not require an account. **The text you translate, translations, text recognized in photos, history, and glossary are processed and stored only on your device, and are never sent to the developer's servers or to third parties.** The developer cannot see your text or translations.

The App does not collect any information about you. It contains no tracking, analytics, or advertising.

## 2. Information the App Handles

### 2.1 Text and translations

- Translation is performed on your device by the translation model included in the App
- Your original text and translations are never sent anywhere for translation

### 2.2 Photos

- Photos you choose are used only to recognize the text in them, on your device
- The App does not save photos or send them anywhere. The recognized text is placed in the input field as text to translate
- The App receives only the photos you pick in the iOS photo picker. It does not ask for access to your whole photo library or the camera

### 2.3 History and glossary

- Translation history and the glossary are stored only on your device (SwiftData). They are not synced to iCloud or uploaded to any server
- You can delete history and glossary entries in the App at any time

### 2.4 Copying and sharing translations

- Translations are copied to the clipboard, or handed to the destination you choose through the share sheet, only when you ask. The App never sends them automatically

### 2.5 Shortcuts

- When you translate from the Shortcuts app, translation also happens on your device. Where the result goes depends on the shortcut you create

## 3. Network Communication

The App does not communicate with any external service. The translation model is included in the App, so there is no model download either.

Downloads and updates of the App are provided through the App Store and are subject to Apple's Privacy Policy.

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties

Because the App does not send your information to the developer's servers, the developer does not share it with third parties. No information is sent to Preferred Networks, Inc., the provider of the translation model.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not send personal information off the device at all. If you are under 18, please use the App with the consent of a parent or guardian ([Terms of Use](terms.md), Article 3).

## 6. Deleting Your Data

- You can delete history and glossary entries in the App at any time
- Deleting the App from your device removes all data stored in it. The developer cannot recover it

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Changes take effect when posted on this page. If we expand the scope of information handled, we will let you know through the App's update notes or similar means.
