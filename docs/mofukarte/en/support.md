# MofuKarte – Support

## About the App

MofuKarte keeps your pet's health records — for dogs, cats, rabbits, birds, hamsters, and more — in one place.

Log weight, vet visits, medications, prevention and vaccines, condition, and notes, and get notified when medicine or prevention is due. Your records stay on your iPhone. No account needed.

## Features

- **6 record types** — Weight, Vet Visit, Medication, Prevention & Vaccines, Condition, and Note. Add costs and photos too
- **Weight chart** — Spot gradual changes early
- **Care reminders** — Daily, weekly, monthly, yearly, or every N days. Tap "Done" to log it and move to the next date
- **Vet Report (Pro)** — Weight trends, prevention, medications, vet visits, and recent condition in an A4 PDF
- **CSV export (Pro)** — Save all records in a format spreadsheet apps can open

## Requirements

- iOS 27.0 or later
- iPhone

## About MofuKarte Pro

The auto-renewable subscription "MofuKarte Pro" unlocks:

- Unlimited pets (free version: 1)
- Unlimited care reminders (free version: 3)
- Weight chart for 1 year or all time (free version: last 3 months)
- Vet Report (PDF)
- CSV export

Monthly and yearly plans are available. Prices vary by country or region and are shown on the purchase screen. The yearly plan may include a one-week free trial for first-time subscribers; when the trial ends, the yearly price is charged automatically.

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period at the same price unless canceled at least 24 hours before renewal.

## FAQ

### Q: I'm not getting notifications

A: Check that notifications are allowed in Settings > Notifications > MofuKarte. Focus modes may also hide notifications. For monthly or yearly reminders, the App schedules the next few occurrences each time you open it, so please open the App from time to time.

### Q: What happens when I tap "Done"?

A: A record with the reminder's name is added, and the reminder moves to its next date. Reminders that don't repeat move to "Finished or Paused."

### Q: Can I log weight in grams?

A: Yes. Switch between "kg" and "g" next to the weight field. Weights under 1 kg are shown in grams.

### Q: I changed phones and my records are gone

A: Records are stored only on the device, so they don't transfer when you change phones or delete the App. Use Pro's CSV export to save your records regularly.

### Q: Will my records be deleted if I cancel Pro?

A: No. Your records, pets, and reminders stay and remain viewable. If you're over the free limits, you just can't add more.

### Q: How do I cancel Pro?

A: Go to the iOS Settings app > your Apple Account > Subscriptions. You can also open the same screen from "Change or Cancel Plan" in the App's Settings tab.

### Q: What if I cancel during the free trial?

A: You won't be charged, and you can use Pro until the trial ends.

### Q: Pro stopped working on my new phone

A: Tap "Restore Purchases" on the Pro screen. If you subscribed with the same Apple Account, your subscription will be restored.

### Q: I'd like a refund

A: Refunds are handled by Apple. Please request one from your App Store purchase history.

### Q: My pet seems unwell

A: MofuKarte is a record-keeping app and cannot diagnose. Please contact your veterinarian. Showing them the Vet Report can help explain your pet's recent condition.
