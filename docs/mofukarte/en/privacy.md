# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "MofuKarte" (the "App") handles your information.

## 1. Our Approach

The App does not require an account. Pet profiles, records, reminders, and photos you enter are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer's servers or to third parties. The developer cannot see your records.

The App contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking.

## 2. Information the App Handles

### 2.1 Information you enter

- Your pet's name, species, breed, sex, birthday, adoption date, notes for the vet, and other notes
- Records such as weight, vet visits, medications, prevention and vaccines, condition, and notes (including dates, titles, costs, and text)
- Care reminders (title, date and time, repeat settings, notes)

This information is stored only on your device (SwiftData). It is not synced to iCloud or uploaded to any server.

### 2.2 Photos

- Photos you choose from your photo library are added to profiles or records
- Photos are selected with the system photo picker. The App never reads your entire photo library
- Photos are resized and stored only on your device

### 2.3 Notifications

- The App schedules local notifications on your device for care reminders
- Notifications are scheduled entirely on your device and do not go through any server
- You can change notification permission anytime in the iOS Settings app

### 2.4 Exported files

- The Vet Report (PDF) and CSV export are created only when you ask for them
- Files are handed to the destination you choose through the share sheet (Files, Mail, printing, etc.). The App never sends them automatically

## 3. In-App Purchases

- MofuKarte Pro is purchased through Apple's StoreKit, and Apple processes payments
- Payment information is managed by Apple. The developer does not receive or store it
- To check your subscription status, the App receives transaction information from Apple, which does not identify you personally

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties

Because the App does not send your information to the developer, the developer does not share it with third parties, except where disclosure is required by law.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not send personal information off the device at all.

## 6. Deleting Your Data

- You can delete individual records and reminders in the App at any time
- You can delete a pet together with all of its records and reminders from the pet's profile screen
- Deleting the App removes all data stored in it. The developer cannot recover it

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes take effect when posted on this page. If we expand the scope of information handled, we will let you know through the App's update notes or similar means.
