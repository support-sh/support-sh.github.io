# Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "Omosa" (the "App") handles user information.

## 1. Our Approach

The App does not require an account. The weight entries you enter are stored only on your device and are never sent to the developer's servers or to any third party. The developer cannot see your entries.

The App contains no tracking or analytics.

## 2. Information the App Handles

- The weights you enter and their dates
- Your display settings (unit kg / lb and chart period)

Weight entries are stored on your device (SwiftData), and the display settings are stored on your device (UserDefaults). Nothing is synced to iCloud or uploaded to any external server.

## 3. Sharing with Third Parties

Because the App does not send your information to the developer's servers, the developer does not provide it to third parties, except where disclosure is required by law.

## 4. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not send personal information off the device at all.

## 5. Deleting Your Data

- You can delete an entry by swiping its row to the left on the History screen
- Deleting the App from your device deletes all data stored in the App. The developer cannot restore it

## 6. Changes to This Policy

The developer may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Changes take effect when posted on this page. If a change expands the information the App handles, we will announce it in the App's update notes or similar.
