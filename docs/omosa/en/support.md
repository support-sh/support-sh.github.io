# Omosa - Support

## About

Omosa is an iOS app that does one thing: log your weight. It opens straight to the input screen — type your weight and tap Save. Your entries are stored only on your iPhone. No account is required.

## Features

- **Log your weight** — one entry per day. Logging again on the same day replaces it
- **Change since last time** — see the change from your previous entry as you type
- **History** — dates and weights, newest first
- **Chart** — your weight trend over 1 month, 3 months, 1 year, or all time (top of the History screen)
- **kg / lb** — tap the unit next to the input to switch

## Requirements

- iOS 27.0 or later
- iPhone

## FAQ

### Q: How do I log or fix a past day?

A: Tap the date at the top of the input screen and choose a day, then enter the weight. You can also tap a row on the History screen to return to the input screen with that day selected.

### Q: How do I delete an entry?

A: On the History screen, swipe the row to the left.

### Q: Can I use pounds instead of kilograms?

A: Tap "kg" next to the input to switch to "lb". Existing entries are shown in the unit you choose.

### Q: My entries disappeared after I changed iPhones

A: Entries are stored only on your device, so they are not carried over when you change devices or delete the App.

### Q: I'm concerned about my weight

A: Omosa is a record-keeping app and does not provide medical advice or diagnosis. Please consult a doctor or other qualified professional.
