# Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the iOS app "KoeMoji" (the "App") handles your information.

## 1. Our Approach

The App does not require an account. **Recorded audio, imported files, transcriptions, summaries, and Dictionary entries are processed and stored only on your device, and are never sent to the developer's servers or to third parties.** The developer cannot see or hear your audio or transcriptions.

The App contains no tracking or analytics.

## 2. Information the App Handles

### 2.1 Microphone audio

- The App captures microphone audio only while you are recording
- Audio is transcribed on your device and saved as an audio file on your device
- You can revoke microphone access anytime in the iOS Settings app

### 2.2 Imported audio and video files

- The App extracts only the audio from the files you choose and transcribes it on your device
- The audio is saved on your device for playback (video images are not saved)

### 2.3 Transcriptions, summaries, and Dictionary

- Transcriptions, summaries, and Dictionary entries are stored only on your device (SwiftData). They are not synced to iCloud or uploaded to any server
- Summaries are created by the on-device language model (Apple Intelligence). The App does not use Apple's cloud processing (Private Cloud Compute)

### 2.4 Exported files

- Text, subtitle, and Word files are created only when you ask for them, and are handed to the destination you choose through the share sheet. The App never sends them automatically

## 3. Network Communication

- **Downloading language speech data**: The speech data for the transcription language is downloaded from Apple's servers through iOS the first time you use it. Your audio and transcriptions are not sent during this download
- **In-app purchases**: KoeMoji Pro is purchased through Apple's StoreKit, and Apple processes payments. The developer does not receive or store your payment information
- Other than the above, the App does not communicate with any external service

## 4. Sharing with Third Parties

Because the App does not send your information to the developer's servers, the developer does not share it with third parties, except where disclosure is required by law.

## 5. Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children. The App does not send personal information off the device at all.

## 6. Deleting Your Data

- You can delete transcriptions in the App at any time (the recorded audio is deleted along with them)
- Deleting the App from your device removes all data stored in it. The developer cannot recover it

## 7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as needed. Changes take effect when posted on this page. If we expand the scope of information handled, we will let you know through the App's update notes or similar means.
